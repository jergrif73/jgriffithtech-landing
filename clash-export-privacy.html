Privacy Policy

JGT Forma Clash Export (Chrome extension) · JGriffith Tech · Effective October 6, 2026

The extension exports the clash list open in Autodesk Forma Model Coordination to files on your computer. It is built to keep your project data on your computer.

What the extension collects

Nothing about you or your models is collected, stored on a server, or shared. When you click Export, the extension reads the clash list and the locations of the clashing elements inside your browser tab, rates the clashes with the rules you set, and writes an Excel report and Navisworks files into a folder you pick. That information never leaves your computer.

What is sent, and to whom

Only one thing. If you activate Pro, your license key and the product ID are sent to Gumroad, the store that sells the license, to confirm the key is valid. That happens when you activate and about once a week after. Gumroad's reply (whether the key is valid, plus a masked copy of the purchase email) is kept only in your browser. Nothing about your models or clashes is ever sent.

What is stored in your browser

  • Your license key and the result of its last check (Chrome extension storage).
  • Your rating rules and export choices, such as the coordinate system (Chrome extension storage).
  • Chrome remembers the last folder you exported to, so the folder window opens there next time.

Remove the key on the extension's options page, reset the rules in its panel, or uninstall the extension, to delete them.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies of our own.
  • No selling or sharing of data. No use of data for anything other than the extension's purpose.
  • No remote code. Everything the extension runs ships inside it.
  • Nothing in Forma is clicked or changed. The extension only reads.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Gumroad under their terms and privacy policy. JGriffith Tech receives the order details Gumroad provides to sellers (such as your email) to support your license.
