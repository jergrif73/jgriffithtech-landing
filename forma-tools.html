Activate Pro

  1. Buy Pro on Gumroad. Got a team code? Enter it at checkout.
  2. Copy the license key from your receipt email.
  3. Click the extension's icon in the Chrome toolbar, paste the key, and choose Activate.
  4. Reload your Forma tab.

Help

  • Nothing happens on the shortcut: reload the Forma tab after installing or updating.
  • "Select a part first" in the Clashes view: click the part itself in the 3D view, then press the shortcut.
  • IDs are only unique per Revit file. Copy ID + Details shows which file each ID came from.
  • Moving to a new computer: remove the key from the old browser's options page, then activate it on the new one. Each key covers up to 5 browsers.
  • Still stuck? Reply to your Gumroad receipt email and it comes straight to me.
