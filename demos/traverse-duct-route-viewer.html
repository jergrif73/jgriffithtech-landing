Verification

Mesh clearance check: passed. Every triangle of the 8 installed SA02 meshes was measured against all 313 pipe centerlines (3⅛" OD). No triangle touches or crosses a pipe. The smallest gap is 0.49" at the 61.97° elbow. Revit's flat mesh facets can sit up to about 0.07" inside the true curved surface, so the conservative figure is about 0.42".

Revit Interference Check: not available. Revit 2024's Interference Check doesn't list the MEP Fabrication Ductwork or Pipework categories (Current Project shows only Ducts and Walls), so it can't check these parts. Navisworks Clash Detective is the native cross-check for fabrication parts.

Connections: all 18 connectors are connected. The joint to the north stub closes with a 0.0000" gap and the directions are parallel. Every part is SA02 on Workset1, with free area held at Ø16 (201 in²). The only rectangular joint is 24" and uses TDC.

How the corridor was found

Each map shows where a Ø16 duct's centerline may sit (light) without touching a pipe (dark), with the beaded radius of 8.19" added to every pipe. Pipes running in X block the Y–Z plane; pipes running in Y block the X–Z plane. A position is legal only if it is light on both maps.
