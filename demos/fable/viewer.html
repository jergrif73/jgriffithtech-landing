How the corridor was found

Each map shows where a Ø16 duct's centerline may sit (light) without touching a pipe (dark), with the beaded radius of 8.19" added to every pipe. Pipes running in X block the Y–Z plane; pipes running in Y block the X–Z plane. A position is legal only if it is light on both maps.
